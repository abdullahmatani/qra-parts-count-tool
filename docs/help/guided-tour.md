# Guided tour and sample project

Two ways to learn the app before starting real work: the **guided tour** walks you through one
complete parts count, and the **sample project** shows a finished study to look round.

## The guided tour

The guided tour is optional. Start it from the start screen with **Take the guided tour**, or at any
time from the app menu on the logo at the top left (**Guided tour**). If a project is open, it is
saved and closed first.

The tour opens a **practice project** in the browser tab: two PEFS sheets of an inlet separator,
already imported, with nothing marked yet. Nothing is written to your computer, so you need no
folder and can experiment freely. The header shows **Practice project** while it is open.

A card at the bottom of the window tells you what to do next, and a pulsing outline shows where to
click. Steps that ask you to do something tick themselves off as soon as you have done it; the
others have a **Next** button. On the drawing, dashed rings mark the symbols the step is about, and
**Show me** zooms the drawing to them.

In about fifteen minutes you will:

1. Open a drawing, move round it and find text on it.
2. Create the segment `IS-01` with its process data.
3. Mark its three ESDVs and set the segments on each side.
4. Mark an end flange where the drain goes to the closed drain.
5. Add the drawing links of the off-page connectors, and follow one.
6. Highlight the segment with **Auto trace**, and outline the separator with a dashed zone.
7. Start the parts count.
8. Count valves and flanges with the equipment bar and Stamp.
9. Let **Find similar symbols** find the rest of the valves.
10. Read the count table, add a note for the checker and mark the segment as counted.
11. Run the pre-export check and export.

Controls on the card:

- **Back** and **Next** move between steps. **Skip** moves on from a step you do not want to do.
- **Learn more** opens the article of this documentation about the step.
- The arrow button folds the card to one line when it is in the way; drag the card by its title to
  move it. The card moves to the other corner of the drawing by itself when it would cover the
  rings of the step, and to the edge of the window while a dialog is open.
- **×** (End the tour) stops the tour. The practice project stays open until you go back to the
  start screen, so you can keep trying things; **Resume the guided tour** in the app menu brings
  the card back at the step it was on.
- If a step belongs to the other stage (for example a counting step while you are back in
  **1 Segments**), the card says so, with a button to go there.

The practice project is gone when you close it. To keep a study, start a real one with
**New project**.

## The sample project

**Try the sample project** on the start screen creates a small, finished study in an empty folder
you choose: two PEFS sheets, two segments (`IS-01` and `IS-02`), the ESDVs between them, a complete
count, signed notes, drawing links between the sheets, and one item deliberately left without a
size, so the pre-export check has something to show.

Unlike the guided tour, the sample is a real project in a real folder: it saves, keeps backups and
exports to its `exports/` folder like any other.
